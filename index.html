About the book

Between hospitals, clinical research at the NIH, and the daily fight for survival, Nicholas Acord shares raw experiences of pain, self-destruction, and awakening — and of resilience, spirituality, and rebirth.

Blending science, Western medicine, ancient practices, and extreme mountain training, Blood Mutant questions the healthcare system, challenges traditional views of illness, and explores what true inner healing requires.

This is not only a medical memoir. It is a story of awareness and human experience — for anyone seeking inspiration, healing, and strength in the face of life’s greatest adversities.
